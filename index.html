Introduction

The current state of security in Nigeria is at it's all time worst. In this article, we will be checking the major causes and how best they could be tackled.

What is going on in Nigeria? And how is it being handled

For the past few years, Nigeria as a country has been bleeding, Economically, Socially and Politically. And the masses arent helping situations either, citizens divided and blinded by tribalism, selfish intent and lack of courage. Politically the political system is a mess, politicians thinking only about how to expand their pocket and powers.

How it is being handled

The current government has been letting been it's people down, they arent taking proper counter measures against bandity and terrorism the country is facing. They also aren't publicly addressing the issue.
